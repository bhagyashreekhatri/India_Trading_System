### 2.2 What "100% sure shot" actually means in trading

There is no system on earth — not Renaissance, not Citadel, not D.E. Shaw — that has a 100% win rate. They have edges. Edges have failure modes.

**Even our best-validated rule has 2 failure modes:**

| Rule | Sample size | Success rate | Failures |
|------|------------:|-------------:|---------:|
| STRONG_GREEN + FHH break | 44 | 100% | 0 (so far) |
| GREEN + FHH break | 38 | 97.4% | 1 |
| GREEN (≥+0.3% alone) | 158 | 72% | 45 days where it failed |

**The day that breaks "100%" is the day you blow up if you size for certainty.**

A real scalper thinks like this:

> "I have a 97% rule. On 100 trades, I expect 3 to fail. If each trade risks ₹1,500 and wins ₹3,000, my expected value is `97 × 3,000 - 100 × 1,500 = ₹141,000 per 100 trades = ₹1,410 per trade`. **But I size every trade as if it might fail.** I never size 5× because I'm confident — confidence costs money when you're wrong."

### 2.3 The math of position sizing for realistic edge

**With the macro+FHH combo (97-100% rule):**

| Scenario | Per-trade risk | Per-trade reward | Win rate | Expected value/trade |
|----------|---------------:|-----------------:|---------:|---------------------:|
| Conservative | ₹1,500 | ₹2,000 | 85% | +₹1,475 |
| Realistic | ₹1,500 | ₹3,000 | 75% | +₹1,875 |
| Optimistic (overconfident) | ₹3,000 | ₹3,000 | 75% | +₹1,500 (similar EV) |

| **Sure-shot mindset** | "no SL needed" | varies | 75% | **catastrophic — single -₹50k day wipes 25 trades of profit** |

**The point:** "100% confidence" doesn't translate to "no risk." It translates to "I size correctly, accept that I'll be wrong 3% of the time, and ensure those 3% don't kill me."

### 2.4 What 30 months of evidence ACTUALLY supports

**ACHIEVABLE (with discipline):**
- 60-70% win rate on filtered trades
- ₹1,000-3,000 net per WINNING trade
- ₹15,000-30,000 net per month on ₹3L capital

**NOT achievable, no matter what system:**
- 100% win rate (anywhere, ever, on any system)
- "Sure shot" without losses
- Linear daily profits with no drawdowns
- Returns above 50%/year without taking outsized risk
- Surviving the next 2024-06-04 election crash if you sized for certainty

### 2.5 The Six Brutal Rules a Pro Scalper Lives By

1. **Every position must have a stop loss BEFORE the trade fires.** No exceptions. The "I'll get out when it hits +₹3000" trader is dead inside 6 months.

2. **Daily loss limit at 2.5% of capital.** When hit, stop trading. Walk away. Tomorrow exists.

3. **Per-trade risk capped at 0.5-1% of capital.** Not 5%, not 10%. The math:
   - With ₹3L capital and 60% WR and 1:2 R:R, you need ~15 trades to recover from a 5-trade losing streak
   - With 10% risk per trade, that 5-trade losing streak wipes 50% of your capital — you can never recover

4. **No revenge trading.** Cooldown after loss (the agent already has this at 45 min).

5. **Size based on EDGE, not on CONFIDENCE.** Even the 100% STRONG_GREEN+FHH rule sizes at standard tier, not 2× standard.

6. **The market will surprise you.** 2024-06-04 came. February 2026 came. March 2026 came (NIFTY -10% in a month). You survive these by being **smaller** than you think you should be.

### 2.6 What I'd tell you if you were my brother

Three things:

1. **The data IS exciting.** STRONG_GREEN+FHH at 100% across 44 events over 30 months is a real, durable edge.

2. **The "100% sure shot" framing is the seed of destruction.** It's how every retail trader I've ever known blew up. Reframe to: "I have a 97% edge. Size to survive the 3%."

3. **₹1,000-3,000 per trade is realistic AS WINS, with disciplined losses of ₹1,500. Net: ₹500-1,500 per trade average across wins+losses.** That's ₹15-30k per month. Real money.
