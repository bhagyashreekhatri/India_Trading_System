#### Why A++ loses — drilldown

| Setup | Exit | n | P&L | Avg R |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| recovery_setup | sl_hit | 8 | -₹8,864 | **-0.66** |

| vwap_reclaim | sl_hit | 1 | -₹1,924 | -1.14 |

The "perfect setup" trades blow past the planned stop by **more than 1R**
on average. That's stop slippage. The cause: when a momentum breakout scores
9+, the structure is visible to every algo on the tape, the position becomes
crowded, and when it fails the unwinding gaps through the stop.

This is a real but second-order leak. NOT recommended for surgical fix on
Day 1 live — would touch the scoring engine which has 10/10 passing tests.
**Action: monitor; if A++ stays negative for another 2 weeks, demote A++
recovery_setup to A+ and require an extra confirmation bar on A++
momentum_breakout.**
